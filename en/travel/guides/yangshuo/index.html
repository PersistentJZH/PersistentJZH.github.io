SOLO TRIP

A smooth trip, even on your own

Rafting

Jinlong Bridge–Jiuxian uses two-person rafts at ¥320 each. Solo travelers can wait to share (10–30 minutes, longer in peak season) or rent the entire raft. Book the 12:15 slot for the best chance of finding a partner.

painted mountains

Avoid hiring a whole car alone. West Street shared cars cost about ¥60/person return, and hotel reception can help find others. A ¥220–300 private car is poor value for one person.

Food

Beer fish is priced by weight. Choose a 1–1.5 jin fish (about 0.5–0.75 kg) for one person, and confirm its weight and unit price first; do not let the restaurant choose a three-jin fish.

Key decision

Can an e-bike reach Qianli Jiangshan?

Yes, but it is not worth the risk. The viewpoint at Shizhaidi in Putao is 29 km from the hotel, with winding mountain roads at the end. There are no streetlights after sunset, while e-bike lights are weak and braking distances long. Use a shared or private car for this section and enjoy the daytime ride.

Recommended · shared car for solo travelersAbout ¥60/person from West Street

Hotel pickup at 15:40, viewpoint at 16:25, descend at 18:35, and return at 19:20. Leaving at 15:40 avoids the 17:00 rush. Ask reception or a West Street car-sharing point to find others. If you prefer not to wait, a private car costs ¥220–300.

Not recommended · e-bike29 km one way · about 1.5 hours

The return trip is 58 km, nearly 90 km including the daytime ride. Battery range, unlit roads, and mountain bends all add risk, unless your bike has a verified range exceeding 100 km.

How could Xianggong Hill fit?It is in Xingping on the east bank, while Qianli Jiangshan is near Putao to the west, 1 hour 10–20 minutes away by car. Leaving after rafting at 14:00 and visiting both puts you at the viewpoint after 17:20, in evening traffic. Choose one: go straight to Qianli Jiangshan at 15:40 on Saturday, or visit Xianggong Hill instead and save Qianli Jiangshan for another trip. Keep Xingping for Sunday.

Daily reminder Waking at 10:00 makes the afternoon tight: raft at 12:15, a 30-minute lunch, ride through Ten-Mile Gallery without stops, and be in the car by 15:40 to avoid the 17:00 rush. Add Xianggong Hill on Sunday, not on the same day as Qianli Jiangshan.

04 / BOOK AHEAD

Book these three things ahead

Rafting and the shared car to Qianli Jiangshan are the only parts with tight timing. Everything else can be arranged on arrival.

Book ahead

Yulong River rafting

The classic Jinlong Bridge–Jiuxian route uses two-person rafts and takes about 90 minutes. Solo travelers can share on arrival or rent a whole raft. Weekend midday slots also fill up; book a 12:00–12:30 slot tonight.

Price
¥320/raft, sharing possible
How to book
Yulong River WeChat account → pier/date/time → real-name registration → collect tickets on site

The guide lists restrictions for children under 1.2 m, adults over 60, pregnant travelers, and people with heart disease or hypertension. Solo travelers can wait to share a raft (10–30 minutes, longer in peak season) or rent the whole raft for ¥320.

Advance booking recommended

Qianli Jiangshan shared / private car

The viewpoint at Shizhaidi in Putao is poorly served by public transport, and finding a return car is difficult. Sharing is easiest for solo travelers; a private car offers more flexibility for two or more.

Price
Shared car about ¥60/person · private car ¥220–300
How to book
Hotel reception / West Street car-sharing point / Didi charter; sharing about ¥60/person

Search for “Yangshuo Qianli Jiangshan large ink-painting photo spot.” Free entry, 08:00–19:00. No food is available on the mountain; bring water.

Optional

Impression Liu Sanjie

An outdoor Li River performance at 20:00, lasting about 70 minutes. If attending on Saturday evening, move Qianli Jiangshan to Sunday morning.

Price
From ¥198
How to book
Official WeChat account / Trip.com / hotel reception, one day ahead

The show is outdoors; bring a raincoat if needed. Taxis queue after the performance, so arrange pickup ahead.

05 / EAT LIKE A LOCAL

Four things to eat in Yangshuo

Beer fish is the signature dish, but avoid restaurants using touts on the main street. Walk into the side lanes for more reasonable prices and food. A 1–1.5 jin fish (about 0.5–0.75 kg) is enough for one person.

BEER FISH

Beer fish

Yangshuo's signature dish: fresh local fish simmered with tomatoes and beer, with tangy, spicy flavors.

Edges of West Street; Xingping Old Town¥80–120/person

RICE NOODLES

Guilin rice noodles

Braised toppings, crispy pork, and sour bamboo shoots. Mix dry first, then add soup—a breakfast staple.

Established street-side noodle shops¥6–10/bowl

STUFFED SNAILS

Stuffed river snails

Chopped snail meat mixed with pork and stuffed back into the shells, with perilla to balance the flavor. Good with a drink.

Farmhouse restaurants¥40–60/dish

TARO PORK

Lipu taro with pork belly

Soft, fluffy taro with rich pork belly, a classic Guangxi banquet dish.

Farmhouse restaurants along the Yulong River¥50–70/dish

06 / BEFORE YOU GO

Check before you leave

Small things, but missing one can make the journey harder.

Bring

  • ID for raft registration
  • Sunscreen / hat
  • Light jacket for 17°C mornings and evenings
  • Sandals / quick-drying trousers
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Power bank
  • ¥20 banknote
  • Motion-sickness medicine

Do

  • Book Yulong River rafting
  • Book a shared / private car to Qianli Jiangshan
  • Check the last railway shuttle
  • Download offline maps
  • Record a video of the rental e-bike
  • Buy travel accident insurance

Avoid

  • Confuse Yangshuo Station with Yangshuo town
  • Ride an e-bike to Qianli Jiangshan after dark
  • Order beer fish at a main-street restaurant using touts
  • Book Li River rafting when you mean Yulong River rafting
